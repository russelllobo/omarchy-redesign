About Omarchy

Omarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, or adjacent hacker culture.

Open to everyone

No invitation-only “official Omarchy” events. Capacity limits are fine.

Community-run

Organizers speak for themselves, not for Omarchy or the Omacom Foundation.

No territorial ownership

Nobody owns a city. Anyone may organize an Omarchy meetup anywhere.

No profiteering

Free is preferred. Cover venue, food, or actual costs — don’t turn the name into an events business.

Sponsors are fine

Local companies can provide space, pizza, and drinks, but sponsorship must be disclosed and the event can’t become a sales pitch.

Behave like civilized adults (with child-like wonder)

Don’t be a jackass. The organizer has final say over removal.

Use the name and logo responsibly

“Omarchy Copenhagen” is fine. Don’t imply you’re Omarchy itself or the Foundation.

Submit it

Put the event on Luma and submit it to the global calendar so everyone can find it.
